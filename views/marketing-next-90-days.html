What we heard (and agree with)

  1. Homepage UX — Long, dense boxes on scroll hurt the look. The homepage look is the strongest visual on the site; other sections should borrow that language (short cards, clear hierarchy, click to go deeper).
  2. Structural SEO — One long homepage cannot carry dozens of primary keywords. Topics need stable, shareable URLs with answer-first content and proper markup.
  3. Workflow stays as agreed — No live partner edits to production. Change requests → owner/developer implements approved items. Blog/content drafts → owner approval → publish.
  4. Compliance floor — Safety, HOS, inspection, and medical-adjacent content must stay educational, cite FMCSA / California rules where relevant, and never undercut legal minimums or sound like medical/legal advice.

Where we are still shaping the approach

You recommended dedicated pillar URLs (e.g. /fatigue-management). We like the blog pattern: an index plus individual shareable pages.

Proposed hybrid (please confirm or adjust):

  • Home & Services stay visual hubs — short teaser cards (title + 1–2 lines + CTA), not full essays in tall boxes.
  • Depth lives on shareable URLs — either existing /blog/{slug} posts we expand, or a small set of new pillar pages when a topic is not a good blog shape (fleet packets, checklists, Social Butterflies hub, etc.).
  • We approve pages case by case — not a bulk ship of 20 thin pages.
  • Goal: keep the Mile 12 Warrior look and trust voice while giving Search Console / AI engines clean destinations.
