What new CDL drivers need in the first 90 days

Rookie truck driver mistakes often come from skipping basics: incomplete pre-trips, fuzzy HOS awareness, and no fatigue plan. This free Tier 1 packet gives you cab-ready checklists and habits so your first 90 days as a truck driver start safer and clearer — educational support, not a substitute for your carrier’s training or FMCSA rules.

  • HOS basics and rest awareness (verify current rules at fmcsa.dot.gov)
  • Pre-trip / post-trip inspection habits
  • Daily wellness and fatigue resets
  • Path into the full 90-Day Onboarding Course

New driver FAQ

Is the New Driver Packet really free?

Yes. Tier 1 stays free forever — view, print, or download with no checkout. The 90-Day Course and other paid tools are separate and clearly labeled.

What should new CDL drivers focus on in the first 90 days?

Inspection habits, HOS awareness (verify current rules at FMCSA), fatigue/rest planning, and a simple wellness routine. This packet supports those basics — your carrier training still governs.

Does this replace carrier training or FMCSA rules?

No. Educational information only — not legal or medical advice. Confirm requirements with FMCSA and your carrier.

How does this connect to the 90-Day Course?

The packet is the printable checklist layer. The course adds modules, knowledge checks, and a certificate path for deeper onboarding.
