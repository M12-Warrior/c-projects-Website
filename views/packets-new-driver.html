What new CDL drivers need in the first 90 days

Rookie truck driver mistakes often come from skipping basics: incomplete pre-trips, fuzzy HOS awareness, and no fatigue plan. This free Tier 1 packet gives you cab-ready checklists and habits so your first 90 days as a truck driver start safer and clearer — educational support, not a substitute for your carrier’s training or FMCSA rules.

  • HOS basics and rest awareness (verify current rules at fmcsa.dot.gov)
  • Pre-trip / post-trip inspection habits
  • Daily wellness and fatigue resets
  • Path into the full 90-Day Onboarding Course
