Family Circle

Community for trucker families

Share tips you have learned over time, lift up another family who needs help, or simply chat when the miles feel long and you are waiting for your driver to reach home or a safe haven.

Welcome — you belong here

This space is for spouses, partners, kids, parents, and friends of drivers — not a duplicate of the driver forum. Start a conversation about home-time routines, worry on stormy nights, creative check-ins, or anything that helps your household thrive while someone you love is on the road.

  • Share tips & tricks — what works for your family at home and on the phone.
  • Offer support — someone may be having a hard week; your kindness matters.
  • Chat through long days — you are not alone while you wait.
  • Share road recipes — labeled lunches and care-package ideas in our Road recipes tab.
